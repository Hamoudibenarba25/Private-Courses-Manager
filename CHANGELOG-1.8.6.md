# v1.8.6 — Student-specific cycle debt transition

- A private/revision cycle balance remains an unpaid balance in its own current cycle after the fourth group session.
- It is shown as previous-cycle debt only after that particular student attends at least one session in the next cycle.
- The Student Debts list, direct debt-payment selector, and cycle financial statements use the same student-specific transition rule.
- A payment made during the fourth session can therefore remain an ordinary receipt in that same cycle and will not be misclassified as previous-cycle debt.
