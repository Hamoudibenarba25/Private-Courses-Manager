# v1.8.5 — Unified previous-cycle debt payments

- The standard private/revision Record Payment form now includes a “Pay a previous-cycle balance” purpose.
- It can target a student's selected previous cycle and caps the amount at that cycle's remaining debt.
- If the original cycle's teacher settlement is still open, the payment is an ordinary private/revision receipt in the original cycle.
- If the teacher settlement is complete, it is classified as previous-cycle debt recovery and allocated to the current cycle.
- The Student Debts page retains its direct payment route and follows the same allocation rule.
- Both routes reduce the same calculated student balance and preserve the original cycle reference on the receipt.
