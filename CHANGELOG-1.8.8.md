# v1.8.8 — Arabic reports and direct CEFR instalments

- Expanded Arabic translation across teacher payments, loan/settlement cards, cycle financial reports, group reports and printable receipts/statements.
- Print windows now inherit Arabic language and right-to-left direction, and translate report labels while preserving school names, class/group names, student names and numeric values.
- Removed the empty “Apply to obligation” selector from the CEFR course-payment workflow.
- CEFR instalments can now be recorded directly by selecting a student and course block, without selecting an obligation. The form shows course fee, paid-to-date and remaining balance, prevents overpayment, records a receipt, and remains compatible with cycle financial accounting.
- Book-charge payments remain obligation-based and separate from CEFR course instalments.
