# v1.8.2 — Full-cycle early payment

- Private-course payment entry now distinguishes the full four-session cycle fee from the amount accrued through attended sessions.
- Students may pay the full cycle price before attending the fourth session. The excess over accrued fees is recorded as prepayment for the remaining session(s) in that same cycle.
- Payment validation caps receipts at the remaining full-cycle price, while keeping older-cycle debts separate.
- Receipts retain the full cycle fee, accrued amount at payment time, and the number of upcoming sessions prepaid.
- Revision-course payment behaviour remains based on its configured per-session fee.
