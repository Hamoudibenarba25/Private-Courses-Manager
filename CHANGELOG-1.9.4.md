# v1.9.4

- Annual insurance charges can be created and paid for students in both Private and CEFR groups.
- Insurance income remains allocated 100% to the school/partner.
- Book charges remain restricted to CEFR groups and allocated 100% to the teacher.
- Insurance is tracked by student and calendar year, independently of teaching-cycle numbering.
- Insurance obligations are visible in Private group workspaces as well as CEFR workspaces.
